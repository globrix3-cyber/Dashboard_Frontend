# Globrixa — Complete Product Overview

> *India's next-generation wholesale marketplace — connecting verified Indian manufacturers with global buyers.*

---

## 1. What is Globrixa?

Globrixa is an online B2B (business-to-business) wholesale marketplace. It is a dedicated platform where Indian manufacturers, artisans, and export houses can list and sell their products in bulk to retailers, importers, and business buyers anywhere in the world.

Unlike consumer marketplaces (Amazon, Flipkart) where individuals buy single products, Globrixa is built for trade — buyers purchase in minimum quantities (cases, dozens, pallets), negotiate prices, and place repeat orders on credit terms.

The closest global comparison is **Faire** (the US wholesale marketplace) or **Alibaba** — but Globrixa is purpose-built for Indian-origin goods, with India-specific compliance (GST), India-specific logistics integrations, and a curated, verified supplier base rather than an open directory.

---

## 2. The Market Opportunity

India has **63 million MSMEs** (Micro, Small & Medium Enterprises) — the largest manufacturing base in the world for handmade, artisan, and agri-processed goods. Despite this scale, most Indian manufacturers:

- Have no professional online presence
- Rely on trade fairs and middlemen to find buyers
- Cannot process international orders without a trading house taking a cut
- Have no digital tools for quoting, contracting, or order tracking

At the same time, global buyers — UK boutiques, US gift shops, European retailers, Australian importers — are actively seeking authentic Indian goods but cannot navigate the fragmented, informal Indian wholesale market.

**Globrixa closes this gap.** It is the professional digital infrastructure that India's manufacturing sector needs to trade globally.

## 5. How the Platform Works — Full User Journey

### Buyer Journey

**Step 1 — Discovery**
The buyer lands on the Globrixa website and browses the product catalogue. They can search by keyword, filter by category (textiles, home décor, food, handicrafts, jewelry, etc.), browse by Indian region (Jaipur, Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Kolkata), or explore curated collections.

No account is required to browse. Prices and supplier details are revealed after registration to incentivise sign-up.

**Step 2 — Registration**
The buyer creates a free account with a business email. The registration flow collects:
- Business type (retailer, importer, online seller, etc.)
- Product categories of interest
- Annual purchasing volume (to qualify for credit terms later)
- Country and currency preference

**Step 3 — Browse and Save**
Logged-in buyers can favourite products, save suppliers, and access wholesale pricing (price per unit at different MOQ tiers). A registered buyer can also see supplier profiles, ratings, and past export history.

**Step 4 — Post an RFQ**
When a buyer knows what they want, they post an RFQ (Request for Quotation):
- Product name and description
- Quantity required (e.g., 500 units)
- Unit type (pieces, kilograms, metres)
- Maximum budget per unit
- Delivery deadline
- Destination country

The RFQ is broadcast to all relevant verified suppliers. Active suppliers respond within 24–48 hours.

**Step 5 — Receive and Compare Quotes**
Suppliers respond with:
- Unit price at requested quantity
- Minimum order quantity (MOQ)
- Lead time (production + shipping)
- Payment terms (advance, 50/50, net-30)
- Validity period of the quote
- Notes on customisation or packaging

The buyer sees all quotes in a structured comparison view.

**Step 6 — Negotiate via Messages**
The buyer can open a direct chat with any supplier. Within the chat, suppliers can send formal Quote Offers (structured cards with price, quantity, lead time) that the buyer can accept or counter with one click.

**Step 7 — Accept and Contract**
When the buyer accepts a quote, a **digital contract** is automatically generated. It includes:
- Full product description and quantity
- Agreed price and total value
- Payment terms and currency
- Delivery deadline and incoterms
- Quality standards and inspection rights
- Warranty and return conditions
- Contract number for legal reference

Both the buyer and supplier sign digitally. The signed contract is stored permanently on the platform.

**Step 8 — Payment and Trade Assurance**
Payment flows through Globrixa's trade assurance system. The buyer's payment is held in escrow and released to the supplier only after the buyer confirms the goods meet the agreed specification. This protects both parties.

**Step 9 — Production and Shipping**
The supplier updates the order status through the production lifecycle:
- `Confirmed` → `In Production` → `Shipped` → `Delivered`

The buyer receives real-time notifications at each stage.

**Step 10 — Reorder**
Satisfied buyers can reorder from the same supplier directly, access their order history, and build a preferred supplier list for repeat trade.

---

### Supplier Journey

**Step 1 — Apply and Get Verified**
The supplier applies with their GST registration number, business documents, and company details. The Globrixa team verifies the application within 2–3 business days. Verified suppliers receive a badge on their profile.

**Step 2 — Build a Catalogue**
The supplier lists their products:
- Product photos (multiple, professional quality encouraged)
- Description, materials, dimensions
- Price tiers (e.g., $8/pc for 50–99 units, $6.50/pc for 100+ units)
- Minimum order quantity
- Lead time for standard and custom orders
- Available for sample orders? Yes/No

Products are reviewed before going live.

**Step 3 — Browse Open RFQs**
The supplier sees a live feed of buyer RFQs relevant to their product categories. Each RFQ shows:
- What the buyer needs
- Quantity and budget
- Deadline
- How many quotes the buyer has already received

**Step 4 — Submit Quotes**
The supplier responds to relevant RFQs with a structured quote. The quote is sent directly to the buyer's dashboard and triggers a notification.

**Step 5 — Win Orders**
When a buyer accepts a quote, the order is created automatically. The supplier manages the order through their dashboard — updating status, communicating with the buyer, uploading shipping documents.

**Step 6 — Grow**
Suppliers build a review history, improve their platform rating, get featured in category listings, and attract repeat buyers. High-performing suppliers get access to premium placement and featured brand status.

## 6. Key Platform Features — In Detail

### Product Catalogue
- 50,000+ products across 24 categories
- Subcategory filtering, attribute search, price range filters
- Product images, specifications, and supplier profile links
- MOQ (minimum order quantity) and price tiers clearly shown
- "New arrivals", "Trending", "Export-ready", and "Bestseller" badges

### RFQ System
- Buyers can post an unlimited number of RFQs
- Each RFQ is visible to all verified suppliers in the relevant category
- Suppliers can see how many competitors have already quoted, encouraging competitive pricing
- RFQ deadline prevents quotes from arriving too late to be useful
- Status lifecycle: `active → closed → cancelled`

### Digital Contracts
- Auto-generated from accepted quote details — no manual drafting
- Unique contract number (format: `CTR-2026-0001`)
- Both parties sign in-browser — no PDF download or DocuSign required
- Full contract terms: incoterms, payment schedule, quality inspection rights, warranty
- Contracts stored permanently and accessible in both parties' dashboards

### Order Management
- Every accepted quote becomes a purchase order
- Order status flows from `Confirmed → In Production → Shipped → Delivered`
- Both parties see the same order record
- Currency, total value, line items, and delivery deadline all tracked
- Admin can view and intervene on any order

### Real-time Messaging
- Every buyer–supplier relationship has its own conversation thread
- Messages appear instantly (Socket.IO, no page refresh needed)
- Suppliers can send formal Quote Offer cards within the chat
- Buyers can accept a quote offer with a single click inside the message
- Unread message badge on the navigation sidebar
- Admin can monitor all conversations for compliance

### Trade Assurance
- Buyer payment is held in escrow
- Released to supplier only after buyer confirmation
- Dispute resolution available if goods don't match contract
- Every transaction is recorded and linked to the digital contract

### GST Compliance
- All suppliers are GST-registered before listing
- Orders generate GST-compliant invoices
- Indian domestic and export invoicing formats supported

### Multi-Currency
- Prices displayed in buyer's preferred currency (USD, GBP, EUR, AED, etc.)
- Orders settled in agreed currency
- Exchange rate transparency at order creation

### Notifications
- Real-time push notifications for: new quotes on your RFQ, new messages, order status changes, contract signature requests, new RFQs matching your categories
- In-platform notification centre + email notifications

## 8. Business Model

Globrixa makes money through a combination of:

| Revenue Stream | Description |
|---|---|
| **Transaction fee** | A percentage of each completed order value (paid by supplier) |
| **Supplier subscription** | Monthly/annual plans for enhanced visibility, priority RFQ alerts, and analytics |
| **Featured listings** | Suppliers pay for promoted placement in category feeds |
| **Trade assurance fee** | A processing fee on escrow-protected payments |
| **Buyer premium plan** | High-volume buyers pay for dedicated account management, net-30 terms, and exclusive supplier access |
| **Data and insights** | Anonymised market intelligence reports for enterprise buyers |

The platform targets **0% to buyers** initially (free to source) and **5–8% commission to suppliers** on completed orders, with the option to reduce the commission rate through a subscription.

## 10. Technology (Non-Technical Summary)

Globrixa runs as a web application — it works in any browser on any device. There is no app to download (though a mobile app is on the roadmap).

**Frontend (what users see):** Built with modern web technology for fast loading, smooth interactions, and a polished feel. Works on desktop, tablet, and mobile.

**Backend (the engine):** A secure server that handles all business logic — logins, storing product data, processing quotes, generating contracts, and delivering real-time updates.

**Database:** All data (products, orders, messages, contracts) is stored in a professional-grade database with daily backups.

**Real-time:** Messages and notifications appear instantly without needing to refresh the page — the same technology used by Slack and WhatsApp Web.

**Security:** Industry-standard encryption for passwords and payment data. All connections are encrypted (HTTPS). Role-based access ensures buyers can't see supplier-only data and vice versa.
