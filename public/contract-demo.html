Opens RFQ Detail — Compares Quotes
Buyer navigates to My RFQs → clicks an active RFQ. All supplier quotes are displayed side-by-side with unit price, total value, lead time, and MOQ for easy comparison.
My RFQs → RFQ Detail
B
Buyer
Selects Supplier & Drafts Contract
Buyer clicks "Select & Contract" on the preferred quote. A contract form opens pre-filled with product, quantity, and agreed price. Buyer adds commercial terms: Incoterms, payment terms, delivery terms, quality standards, inspection rights, warranty, and dispute resolution.
Auto-filled from quote
S
System
Contract Created — Buyer Auto-Signed
Contract is assigned a unique number (e.g. CTR-2026-1000). Buyer's signature is recorded automatically as the initiating party. Status set to Pending Supplier. Supplier receives an in-app notification.
CTR-2026-1000 generated
S
Supplier
Reviews Contract in Detail
Supplier opens the Contracts section and sees "ACTION REQUIRED" badge. Full contract is displayed — trade details, commercial terms, delivery schedule, quality standards, and legal framework — all in one page.
Contracts → Pending Supplier
S
Supplier
Signs or Cancels
Supplier clicks "Sign Contract" and confirms via a legal acknowledgement modal. Contract status immediately updates to Active. Both parties receive a notification. Buyer can then see the contract is fully executed.
Status → Active
B
Buyer
Places Order from Active Contract
The "Place Order" button becomes available. Buyer confirms order placement. The contract is marked Completed, the RFQ is closed, and the order appears in both the buyer's and supplier's order management with status "Confirmed".
Order created → My Orders
